# Software Requirements Specification

## Overview

This document outlines the requirements for the Vehicle & Boat
Maintenance Tracker. The application will allow users to manage
their vehicles and boats, keep track of maintenance history,
and schedule future maintenance. These requirements describe
what the system needs to do and how it should perform.

## Functional Requirements

### 1. Vehicle Management

1. **FR1:** The system shall allow users to add vehicles
   and boats by entering information such as make, model,
   year, and mileage or engine hours.

### 2. Maintenance Tracking

1. **FR2:** The system shall allow users to add maintenance
   records that include service dates, maintenance types,
   costs, and notes.

2. **FR3:** The system shall allow users to view, edit,
   and delete maintenance records for their vehicles
   and boats.

### 3. Maintenance Scheduling

1. **FR4:** The system shall allow users to schedule
   upcoming maintenance based on a date, mileage,
   or engine hours.

## Non-Functional Requirements

### 1. Compatibility

1. **NFR1:** The system shall support the latest versions
   of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge on desktop
   and mobile devices.

### 2. Performance

1. **NFR2:** The system shall display vehicle information
   and maintenance records within three seconds under
   normal operating conditions.

### 3. Security

1. **NFR3:** The system shall require users to authenticate
   before accessing their saved vehicle and maintenance
   information.

2. **NFR4:** The system shall ensure that users can only
   access and modify vehicle and maintenance records
   associated with their own accounts.
