# Speidel Software

The Vehicle & Boat Maintenance Tracker is a native iOS app, built with Swift and SwiftUI, that gives vehicle and boat owners one organized place to manage their cars, trucks, boats, and other vehicles. Users can store key details such as make, model, year, mileage, and engine hours, record maintenance and repair history with notes and service details, and see upcoming or overdue maintenance based on dates, mileage, or engine hours. By replacing scattered paper receipts and memory with a single, secure record, the system helps owners avoid missed maintenance, prevent expensive repairs, extend the life of their assets, and easily provide a complete service history when selling.
