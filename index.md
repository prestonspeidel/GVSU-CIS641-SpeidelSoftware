# Vehicle & Boat Maintenance Tracker

### CIS 641 - Systems Analysis and Design

**Developer:** Preston Speidel

## Project Overview

The Vehicle & Boat Maintenance Tracker is a web application
I'm planning to build that helps people keep track of
maintenance for their cars, trucks, boats, and other vehicles.

The application will let users add their vehicles, record
maintenance and repairs, track costs, and see when upcoming
maintenance is needed.

The goal is to make it easier to keep everything organized
in one place instead of trying to remember when something
was last serviced.

## Planned Features

- Add and manage cars, trucks, boats, and other vehicles
- Track mileage and engine hours
- Record maintenance and repair history
- Store maintenance costs and notes
- Schedule upcoming maintenance
- View maintenance history for each vehicle
